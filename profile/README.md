## About Us

Yiliphoton is a hard-tech company focused on 3D SPAD digital LiDAR. We develop proprietary SPAD-ASIC chips and provide one-stop perception solutions for intelligent driving and embodied robotics.

Our full-stack capabilities span optical design, hardware circuitry, low-level algorithms, and automotive-grade mass production — delivering high-performance, cost-effective LiDAR products through a differentiated technology roadmap.

## 关于我们

杭州一力光子科技有限公司是专注 3D SPAD 数字化激光雷达的硬科技企业。自研 SPAD-ASIC 芯片及一站式感知解决方案，聚焦智能驾驶、具身机器人两大赛道。

公司打通光学设计、硬件电路、底层算法、车规量产全产业链，依托差异化低成本技术路线，打造国产高性能、高性价比激光雷达供应商。
